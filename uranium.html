Guhan Nagarajan's Secret Uranium Enterprise

By Dr. Sunay Miduthuri, expert correspondent at SJC News.

Published Jan 30, 2026.


Recently, I received a letter from my good friend Guhan Nagarajan, informing me on the sources of his well-known "secret" uranium processing facility. The facility, which doubles as a basement as its day job, is also home to a host of children, about whom Mr. Nagarajan swears he knows nothing.

Deeper investigation has revealed that he has already begun to acquire the uranium, and is currently manufacturing a nuclear bomb. Expert estimates suggest it will be ready by the end of February.

"It's really concerning," says local Sean C. "The poor kids in Guhan's basement might be imperiled due to the local radiation." When asked how he knew about the kids in Nagarajan's basement, Sean declined to comment.

This is made even more concerning by the fact that Nagarajan has expressed his willingness to launch the bomb once he finishes its construction. He himself argues that this possession is immoral, and morality expert Dr. D. Ding would certainly agree.

"I mean, regardless of whether or not he intends to launch it --- although I believe he will indeed launch it --- we have to face the fact that his possession itself is immoral." Ding argues that because we cannot be sure of the consequences of this possession, we must take Nagarajan's word for it that he really does intend to launch the bomb, and quarantine it.

Local authorities are looking into how he acquired this uranium. Currently, leading detectives believe that it was crowdsourced and mined somewhere in Western Kazakhstan.
