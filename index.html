"I Would Launch a Nuke"

Guhan Nagarajan on Nuclear Control

Published Jan 8, 2026

I, Guhan Nagarajan, assert that if I were ever to come into possession of nuclear weapons—whether tactical or strategic—I would inevitably use them. This hypothetical willingness to deploy such weapons implies that my possession of nuclear arms would itself constitute a moral wrong. Because nuclear weapons are capable of causing indiscriminate, large-scale destruction and irreversible human suffering, entrusting them to an individual who would foreseeably authorize their use is ethically indefensible. Therefore, the immorality does not lie solely in the act of launching nuclear weapons, but in the very act of allowing someone like Guhan to possess them in the first place. The conclusion follows that the possession of nuclear weapons by Guhan is immoral, as it creates an unacceptable risk of catastrophic harm.

Guhan Nagarajan is a high schooler at the Harker School in California. When he's not writing articles about nuclear bombs, he likes to launch them.
